# Suite K directional registration: measured fixed outcome

**All four attempts remain unresolved.** The best transforms visually localize
the corresponding enlarged suite in all four indexed floor locations, including
the expected mirrored pair, but fail the predeclared numerical gates. This is
useful approximate alignment evidence, not accepted registration, verified room
geometry or a room-detection accuracy result. No outline or approval was copied.

The [immutable declaration](DIRECTIONAL_REGISTRATION.md) keeps the old page-25
crop, page-18 four ROIs, printed .4 scale and eight D4 states. It replaces SIFT's
descriptor prerequisite with oriented axis-run distance matching. Nineteen
fabricated tests passed; independent preflight reviewed all final five source
hashes, pixel transforms, held-out groups, clutter/ambiguity gates and producer
joins. Two pre-run fixture defects were repaired: a wide band could be chunked
into fit/check, and nondivisible input dimensions could export a scale different
from the raster resize. A displayed-peak regression prevents a rejected best-fit
transform being captioned as a different supported one. No gate or source was
changed after actual execution began.

The single CPU attempt took **6.276 s overall / 5.727 s child** with **463.117 MiB
peak child RSS**, well within its 120-second declaration. Runtime: Python 3.12.14,
NumPy 2.5.2, SciPy 1.18.1, Pillow 12.3.0. Torch was not imported. No new PDF render,
GPU operation, model, provider call, training target or protected/new-once corpus
entered the study.

before input inspection. Every displayed placement contains its indexed anchor,
but that is only crop-frame containment. Reverse axis support is .488–.621 for
these best fits; three also fail the long unexplained-run gate. Each orientation's
minimum exceeds the fit gate, so there are zero eligible local minima and the
32 retained peaks are explicitly diagnostic best failures. All 32 full translation
cost surfaces are preserved, not merely the displayed candidates. No peak budget
overflow, input failure or processing failure occurred; this was a geometry-check
rejection, not a claim that correspondence is absent.

The source supplied 127 horizontal and 115 vertical observable run families,
partitioned 64/63 and 58/57 between fit/check. Target ROIs supplied 1116, 1224, 1241
and 1251 axis-run candidates. These counts include dimension lines, fixture edges
and other drawing ink; they are not wall or room counts. Disjoint source runs
are not independent physical-wall annotations.

## Visual falsification and practical consequence

The original template and all four source-faithful overlays were inspected.
The large exterior/partition wall chains and stepped suite footprint visibly
land near their corresponding floor drawing, with mirrors on instances 1/2.
This provides inspectable approximate placements where SIFT returned no
supported registration. Interior fixture edges, dimensions, door/partition offsets and faint
floor ink still disagree. The overlays show fit evidence in cyan and withheld
evidence in magenta; neither color means a certified physical wall.

The floor and enlarged view also depict different annotation/fixture detail.
Treating every long axis run as structural evidence penalizes those differences
and can reward unrelated grids. This trial does **not** prove that numerical
gates are too strict or that the displayed transforms are correct. No residual
threshold was loosened, no feature subset was selected from these outcomes and
no interior geometry was transferred. Known-scale matching is worth keeping as
review assistance: a future separately declared study could use a
reviewed wall/junction or boundary subset, hold out separate structural checks,
and compare competing detail/revision hypotheses. The present evidence cannot
justify automatic copying or whole-floor detection.
