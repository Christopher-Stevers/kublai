# Raster2Seq real-sheet development probes, 2026-10-02

Three fixed local inference probes completed. Cropped CubiCasa256 proposals look
more like drawn rooms than its whole-sheet result, but faint interior walls,
circulation, false partitions and invalid rings remain. Gamma contrast helps
some local details and harms others. The official Raster2Graph512 profile misses
and merges more visible spaces despite generating fewer invalid polygons. All
three are rejected as automatic production room detection. No full real-sheet
ground truth or room accuracy score exists for these probes.

## Measured counts, separate from correctness

Cells are **model polygon proposals / strictly valid polygons**. Valid means a
nonempty Shapely polygon with a valid ring; it does not mean a correct room.
Counts include outdoor and incorrectly classified regions. Line elements are
excluded. There is no silent repair of self-intersections or crop clipping.

## Source-overlay visual findings

All 21 emitted overlays were inspected over the original source pixels. These
are qualitative observations; no manual answer-key polygons were fabricated.

- **Full sheet:** Raw256 collapses parking/common circulation into a giant
  region and leaves many interior spaces out. Gamma does not recover them.
  Raw512 produces an especially clear false giant bedroom across parking and
  part of the north/east structure.
- **West upper:** Raw256 follows several strong exterior/unit boundaries and
  some small enclosures, but merges gray interior bathroom/room partitions.
  Gamma reduces some invalid rings and separates a local circulation pocket,
  while preserving those interior merges. Raw512 leaves portions at the top
  unproposed and labels stairs/units as unrelated semantic classes.
- **West lower:** Gamma recovers a small enclosed pocket near the upper center
  and improves some separations, but still merges large gray-wall interiors and
  introduces a false partition around the lower revision-cloud area. Raw512
  merges central circulation with an adjacent unit and omits small rooms.
- **North common:** Raw256 offers numerous small partitions around visible
  service/stair enclosures but includes false parking/exterior regions. Gamma
  loses several small partitions and adds invalid shapes. Raw512 merges the
  washroom/service cluster and stairs into only a few larger regions.
- **East upper:** Gamma separates more of the lower kitchen/interior area, but
  misses the small gray-wall bathrooms and retains false exterior regions.
  Raw512 omits portions and merges the center stair/circulation with a unit.
- **West local:** The raw256 close-up resolves more gray interior divisions
  than the wider crop. Gamma adds some top-right separation but spuriously
  splits the central stair enclosure. Raw512 leaves the upper-left gray-wall
  interior unproposed and merges the top-right interior.
- **North local:** Raw256 separates more service pockets than the wider crop;
  gamma separates additional visible small bathroom/service enclosures, but
  adds a false circulation subdivision. Raw512 retains large merges and
  misses much of the small-room group.

Semantic predictions are unreliable: CubiCasa calls actual parking Garage in
one raw crop, while Raster2Graph predicts a giant bedroom on the whole sheet.
The newer overlays show class names and `INVALID` explicitly. Invalid proposals
remain in raw JSON for diagnostics, flagged as quarantined for geometry use.
There are no model confidence or room-completeness claims.

## Next decision

Do not replace the current detector with either checkpoint or enable gamma
globally. The observed local crop improvements support a bounded next design:
context-aware native-resolution proposals, trained/adapted on verified drawing
styles and physical partitions rather than relying on whole-sheet thumbnails.
They do not prove that this particular architecture will reach completeness.

The next bottleneck is independently reviewed real-sheet annotations, including
faint walls, genuine doors, hallways, courtyards and false fixture partitions.
Resolve the physical-room versus suite/common target policy before labeling and
scoring. Pin the PDF/page/hash and policy; keep whole source drawings disjoint
between adaptation and evaluation; report misses, false rooms, merges/splits,
complete-floor success and correction effort. Use partial positives only with
unknown masks, and require completeness attestation for full-room recall.
Pretrained proposals may help offline annotation, but geometrically valid output
alone cannot certify the business requirement of finding every space.

Training-data rights and model-card licensing are distinct, as documented in the
README. Neither a CubiCasa-based benchmark nor an LIFULL-derived dataset should
be silently adopted as commercially cleared real-sheet training data. No new
training, broad checkpoint search, production change or background trial is
promised by this report.
